Native fixes and AYN Thor retest — November 18, 2026

FRLG+ and Pokémon Classic passed the targeted Thor checks and qualify for this follow-up. FireRed and the PokéDaisy APK retain their previously published builds. The initial graphics-preview report remains a historical record of its earlier binaries.

What changed

  • FRLG+ 1.5.1: the native Bag now has Items, Medicine, Key Items, Held Items and Balls tabs. Scroll state and touch IDs cover all five pockets, and the snapshot rejects invalid pocket indices. TM Case and Berry Pouch retain their existing separate screens.
  • Classic 1.5.1: Kanto's repeated forest and standalone tree quadrants now use the correct tree geometry and matching art, closing the missing ground/canopy cells. The battle category image is resolved from packed assets before indexing it; its sheet and palette are now included in the asset index. This removes the corrupted pixels beside PP.

Automated checks and limits

FRLG's strict C99 host regression covers pocket labels/order and touch boundaries. Classic's tree regression covers complete ground/crown geometry, chunk seams, negative map origins and tileset cases. Its battle regression exercises the actual patched function with all three category indices and missing/malformed assets. CI native builds and shipping audits passed. Classic's final recipe contains the 384-byte category image and 32-byte palette. Web archive manifests and file hashes passed validation.

This follow-up targets the reported defects and early-game regressions; it is not a full-playthrough test. Other Classic 2D ground speckles and previously measured native battle pacing remain outside this fix. Empty Held Items and Balls tabs were checked on the Thor, but populated late-game inventories and every special Bag screen were not exercised. The existing climate presets and performance measurements remain documented in the initial report; no new performance improvement is claimed.
